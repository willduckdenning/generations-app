---
name: Generations product intent
description: User-provided audience and role-based accessibility requirements for Generations.
---

Generations is a family communication app for grandparents, parents and grandchildren, not a public social network.

**Why:** The user's product brief emphasizes keeping generations connected without complex social platforms.

**How to apply:** Keep family updates, messages, photos and voice notes central. Role selection should shape the experience. Parents and grandchildren have richer communication controls; grandparents need an ultra-simplified experience with two large primary actions, Main Home and Picture Taking, to reduce navigation errors and frustration.

The supplied website uses a giraffe wearing sunglasses as its mascot.

**Why:** This is part of the existing product identity supplied by the user.

**How to apply:** Preserve that recognizable identity when extending branding, unless the user requests a change.

The intended full product uses phone-number accounts and lets members create or join a family with a room code, then text and call other members.

**Why:** The user described these as final-product capabilities; current demo flows are placeholders, not production messaging or telephony.

**How to apply:** Clearly distinguish prototypes from live services. For production, use real authentication and shared family membership, and add an authorized messaging/calling service rather than implying that local-only messages or simulated calls reach relatives.
